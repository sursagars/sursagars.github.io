About Us

Welcome to our suite of professional AI image tools. We believe that powerful, privacy-respecting technology should be accessible to everyone, not just those who can afford expensive subscriptions.

Our Mission

To provide free, professional-grade AI tools that respect user privacy and deliver enterprise-quality results without compromises.

What We Offer

  • AI Background Remover: Remove backgrounds from images using advanced AI technology. Perfect for e-commerce sellers, designers, and professionals who need clean product photos or headshots.
  • Smart Image Compressor: Compress images up to 80% smaller without losing quality. Includes platform-specific presets for Reddit, Instagram, websites, and email.

Why We're Different

Privacy-First: Unlike most online tools that upload your images to their servers, our tools process everything locally in your browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly. Your images never leave your device.

No Watermarks: Many "free" tools add watermarks to your images. We don't. Your work is yours, completely clean and ready to use.

Truly Free: No hidden costs, no premium tiers, no subscriptions. All features are available to everyone, forever.

How We Sustain This

We keep these tools free through optional donations from users who find them valuable. We may also display non-intrusive ads to help cover hosting costs. All core functionality remains completely free.

Our Technology

  • Modern compression algorithms for optimal file sizes

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

Our Privacy Commitment

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Unlike most online tools, we don't collect, store, or process your images on our servers. Everything happens in your browser.

What Information We Collect

Images and Files: NONE. All image processing happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your images never leave your device or get uploaded to our servers.

Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, or any personal information. You don't need to create an account or sign in.

Usage Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand how people use our tools (page views, which tools are popular, etc.). This data is anonymized and does not include any image data or personal information.

How Our Tools Work

Background Remover: Uses ONNX Runtime to run AI models directly in your browser. The AI model downloads once and runs locally. Your images are processed using your device's CPU/GPU.

Image Compressor: Uses HTML5 Canvas API and JavaScript to compress images. All processing happens in your browser's memory.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics: For anonymous usage statistics
  • Google AdSense: For displaying ads (optional revenue)
  • PayPal: For processing donations (if you choose to donate)

Cookies

  • Remembering user preferences (like tool settings)

No cookies are used to track your images or personal data.

Data Security

Since your images never leave your browser, there's no risk of data breaches or unauthorized access to your files. This is the most secure way to process images online.

Children's Privacy
