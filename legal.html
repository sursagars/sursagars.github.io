Our Technology

🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

Our Core Privacy Principle: Your images never leave your device. All processing happens locally in your browser. We cannot see, access, or store your images because they never reach our servers.

Our Privacy Commitment

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Unlike most online tools, we don't collect, store, or process your images on our servers. Everything happens in your browser. This isn't just a policy—it's how our technology works.

What Information We Collect

Images and Files: NONE

All image processing happens locally in your browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly. Your images never leave your device or get uploaded to our servers. We have no ability to see or access your images.

Personal Information: NONE

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any personal information. You don't need to create an account, sign in, or provide any information to use our tools.

Usage Analytics: ANONYMIZED ONLY

We use Google Analytics to understand how people use our tools (page views, which tools are popular, user flow, etc.). This data is:

  • Completely anonymized
  • Does not include any image data
  • Does not include personal information
  • Used only to improve our tools
  • Aggregated (we can't identify individual users)

How Our Tools Work

Background Remover:

Uses ONNX Runtime to run AI models directly in your browser. The AI model downloads once (cached by your browser) and runs locally on your device. Your images are processed using your device's CPU/GPU. The processed images remain in your browser's memory and are never sent to any server.

Image Compressor:

Uses HTML5 Canvas API and JavaScript to compress images. All processing happens in your browser's memory. The compressed images are generated locally and never leave your device.

💡 How to Verify: Open your browser's network tab (F12 → Network) while using our tools. You'll see that after the initial page load and AI model download, there are NO network requests when you process images. This proves your images aren't being uploaded.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics: For anonymous usage statistics (page views, clicks, user flow)
  • Google AdSense: For displaying ads (optional revenue to cover hosting)
  • UPI/Buy Me a Coffee: For processing donations (only if you choose to donate)
  • GitHub Pages: For hosting our website
  • CDN Services: For loading libraries like ONNX Runtime, Font Awesome

Cookies

We use minimal cookies for:

  • Google Analytics: Tracking visits and understanding user behavior (anonymized)
  • User Preferences: Remembering your tool settings (like quality slider position, format preferences)

What we DON'T use cookies for:

  • Tracking your images
  • Collecting personal data
  • Selling data to third parties
  • Advertising tracking (beyond basic AdSense)

Data Security

Since your images never leave your browser, there's no risk of:

  • Data breaches from our servers (we don't have your data)
  • Unauthorized access to your files
  • Man-in-the-middle attacks during upload
  • Data leaks or hacks

This is the most secure way to process images online. Your files are as safe as your local device.

Data Retention

We don't retain any of your image data because we never receive it. The only data we retain is anonymized analytics from Google Analytics, which is automatically deleted after 26 months per Google's policy.

Your Rights

Since we don't collect personal data, there's nothing to delete, export, or access. You have complete control over your data because it never leaves your device.

Children's Privacy

Our tools are suitable for all ages. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13. Since we don't collect any personal data, our tools are COPPA-compliant.

International Users

Our tools work the same way regardless of your location. Since all processing is local, your data never crosses borders. This makes us compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations.
